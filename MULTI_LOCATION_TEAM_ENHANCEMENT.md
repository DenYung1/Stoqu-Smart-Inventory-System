# Multi-Location & Team Management Enhancement

## Current State vs Enterprise Features

### Current Capabilities ✅
- Single-user/single-location inventory management
- Admin dashboard for user management
- Subscription-based access control
- Cross-device synchronization

### Enterprise Enhancement Opportunities 🏢

#### **1. Multi-Location Support**
- **Location Management**: Add/manage multiple business locations
- **Location-Specific Inventory**: Track stock per location
- **Inter-Location Transfers**: Move inventory between locations
- **Location Performance**: Sales and profit by location
- **Centralized Reporting**: Combined reporting across all locations

#### **2. Team Management & Permissions**
- **User Roles**: Manager, Cashier, Stock Clerk, Viewer
- **Granular Permissions**: Control access to specific features
- **Activity Logging**: Track who did what and when
- **Approval Workflows**: Require approval for certain actions
- **Team Performance**: Individual and team metrics

#### **3. Advanced User Roles**

##### **Store Manager**
- Full location access
- Staff management
- Reporting and analytics
- Supplier management

##### **Cashier**
- Sales entry only
- Basic product lookup
- Limited reporting
- No price changes

##### **Stock Clerk**
- Inventory management
- Stock movements
- Receiving goods
- No sales access

##### **Viewer**
- Read-only access
- Basic reports
- No data modification
- Dashboard viewing

#### **4. Collaboration Features**
- **Team Chat**: Built-in messaging
- **Task Assignment**: Assign inventory tasks
- **Shift Management**: Track work schedules
- **Handover Notes**: Shift transition communication
- **Team Notifications**: Role-based alerts

#### **5. Enterprise Reporting**
- **Multi-Location Dashboards**: Consolidated view
- **Team Performance Metrics**: Individual productivity
- **Cross-Location Analytics**: Comparative analysis
- **Management Reports**: Executive summaries
- **Compliance Reporting**: Audit trails and compliance

#### **6. Franchise Support**
- **Franchise Management**: Multi-franchise oversight
- **Standardized Catalogs**: Consistent product offerings
- **Franchise Reporting**: Performance across franchises
- **Central Procurement**: Bulk purchasing coordination
- **Brand Consistency**: Standardized pricing and procedures

## Business Impact
- **Market Expansion**: Target larger businesses
- **Higher Revenue**: Enterprise pricing tiers
- **Competitive Moat**: Complex feature set difficult to replicate
- **Customer Retention**: Enterprise customers have lower churn

## Implementation Strategy
1. **Phase 1**: Basic multi-user support within single location
2. **Phase 2**: Multiple locations for single business
3. **Phase 3**: Advanced permissions and workflows
4. **Phase 4**: Franchise and enterprise features

## Technical Considerations
- **Database Schema**: Major restructuring required
- **Authentication**: Role-based access control
- **UI/UX**: More complex interface requirements
- **Performance**: Handling larger data volumes
- **Pricing**: New subscription tiers needed
